How to read the moves

RTurn the right face clockwise, as if you are looking at it. R'Prime: turn it counterclockwise. R2Turn it twice. U D F B LTop, bottom, front, back and left faces. r fLowercase: turn two layers together. MThe middle slice, turned like L. ( )A chunk to learn as one move, like (R U R' U').

Your 20-minute day

  1. 5 min flashcards. Practice tab, only the ones you're learning.
  2. 10 min pairs only. Scramble, make the cross, solve the 4 pairs slowly without stopping. Then do it again.
  3. 5 min timed solves. Timer tab. Watch your average of 5, not your best.

Learn 2 new algorithms a day at most. Know them well before adding more.

How long it really takes

Algorithms are only part of it. Pairs and looking ahead make up most of the time you save.

Week 1–2Learn the 4 finish algorithms and the 3 easiest pairs. Sub-40 is realistic.

Month 2–4Look for the next pair while you solve the current one. Sub-30.

Competition guide

Your first official competition

Official speedcubing results come from World Cube Association (WCA) competitions. Anyone can enter, and there are competitions all over the Philippines. Here is what happens and what the rules say.

Find competitions in the PhilippinesRead the full regulations

How a competition day goes

In order, from signing up to seeing your name in the rankings.

  1. Register online. Pick a competition on the WCA website, choose your events and pay the fee. Popular ones fill up fast.
  2. Check the schedule. Each event has rounds and groups. Know when your group is called.
  3. Check in. You get a name tag and scorecards. First-timers may be asked for an ID.
  4. Wait to be called. Put your cube on your scorecard in the waiting area when your group is up.
  5. Solve. A judge runs each attempt with a Stackmat timer and writes your time on the scorecard.
  6. Help out. Between your groups you may judge, scramble or run cubes. Everyone helps.
  7. See your results. After the competition you get a WCA ID, and your times appear on your WCA profile with world, continent and national ranks.

One official solve, step by step

Practice exactly this in the Timer tab with WCA inspection on.

1Cover off, inspection starts

The judge lifts the cover. You have 15 seconds to look at the cube. You may pick it up and rotate it, but you may not turn any layer.

2The judge calls 8 and 12

"Eight seconds" and "Twelve seconds" are reminders only.

3Start on the mat

Put the cube down, rest your fingers on the timer pads, palms down. The solve starts when your hands leave the pads.

4Stop with both hands

Let go of the cube first, then stop the timer with both hands. Stopping while holding the cube is +2.

5The judge checks it

Each layer may be off by up to 45°. One move away from solved is +2. More than that is DNF.

6Sign the scorecard

Check the time the judge wrote, then sign. Once you sign, you agree with the result.

Penalties to know

Start after 15 s of inspection (before 17 s)+2
Start at 17 s of inspection or laterDNF
Turning a layer during inspectionDNF
Cube one move away from solved+2
Cube two or more moves awayDNF
Stopping the timer while holding the cube+2
Starting with fingers off the pads+2

DNF means "did not finish". The attempt counts as the slowest possible time.

How your result is worked out

Average of 5

Used for 3x3, 2x2, 4x4, 5x5, OH and most events. Drop the best and worst, then average the middle 3. One DNF counts as your worst time. Two DNFs make the average a DNF.

10.21 · 9.80 · 11.05 · 13.40 · 10.74 → 10.67
Mean of 3

Used for 6x6, 7x7, blindfolded and fewest moves. The average of all 3 attempts.

Cutoffs and time limits

First rounds often have a cutoff: if neither of your first 2 solves beats it, you stop after 2. A time limit ends any solve that goes too long, and it counts as DNF.

Rounds

The fastest people from each round move on, for example the top 75%. The final decides the winner, by average.

What to bring

  • Your cubes for every event you entered, cleaned and lubed the week before, not the day before
  • A pen, water and a snack. Days can be long
  • An ID if it is your first competition
  • A cube bag or box with your name on it

Practice like it is real

  • Turn on WCA inspection in the Timer settings and always use it
  • Set the hold time to 0.55 s, like a real Stackmat
  • Watch your ao5, not your best single. That is what ranks you
  • Do your last 20 practice solves with no warm-up, like your first solve of the day
